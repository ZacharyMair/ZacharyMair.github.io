Personal project

Automatic Energy-Saving Light Switch

A cover that fits over a normal toggle switch, so nothing has to be rewired. A motion sensor checks whether anyone is in the room. If it's been empty long enough, a servo arm flips the switch off, and a small LCD shows an estimate of the energy saved.

What I did
Programmed and simulated the control system in Tinkercad, modelled the servo arm, enclosure and lid in SolidWorks for 3D printing, then built the circuit on a breadboard.
Built with
Arduino, PIR sensor, servo, LCD, SolidWorks, 3D printing
