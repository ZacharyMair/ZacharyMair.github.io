Things I've built

Automatic energy-saving light switch

I kept leaving my lights on, and I didn't want to rewire anything. So I designed a cover that fits over a normal toggle switch. A motion sensor watches the room, and once it's been empty long enough, a servo arm flips the switch off. A small LCD keeps a running estimate of the energy saved.

I wrote and tested the control logic in a Tinkercad simulation first, then modelled the servo arm, enclosure and lid in SolidWorks for 3D printing.

Built with Arduino, PIR motion sensor, servo, LCD, SolidWorks, 3D printing
